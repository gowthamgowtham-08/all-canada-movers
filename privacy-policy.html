Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you request a quote, contact us, or otherwise communicate with Top End Van Lines through our website, we may collect information necessary to respond to your request and provide our moving services.

This may include:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Origin and Destination Addresses or Postal Codes
  • Moving Dates
  • Move Type
  • Details about your household, office, vehicle, piano, or other relocation requirements
  • Any additional information you voluntarily provide through our forms or communications

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Providing moving estimates and quotes
  • Coordinating moving services, transportation, logistics, and scheduling
  • Communicating with you about your inquiry or relocation
  • Responding to questions and requests
  • Managing and improving our services and website
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of our website and business operations

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We may share personal information with trusted service providers that assist us in operating our website, processing inquiries, communicating with customers, and providing or coordinating moving services.

These service providers may include website hosting, form-processing, communication, technology, payment, transportation, or other service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our business and provide requested services.

We only share information where reasonably necessary for the relevant business purpose or to comply with applicable legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal information.

Where third-party service providers process information on our behalf, we expect them to use the information only for the purposes for which it was provided and as required by applicable law.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with applicable legal or regulatory requirements.

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete or dispose of it.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about how we handle your personal information, please contact our team.
